Meet the Teacher

A short introduction

A student of Islamic Creed (al-‘Aqidah), Shafi’i Jurisprudence (Fiqh), Prophet Narrations (al-Hadith) and the Arabic language, seeking to benefit others as I have been benefited. My educational approach is rooted in the Hadith of our Master Jibril (عليه السلام), which outlines the three divisions of our religion: Islam (Religious Practice), Iman (Belief), and Ihsan (Spiritual Purification). Mastery of these three essential sciences is mastery of the religion itself.

Aqidah

Introductory 'Aqidah

Explore the core tenets of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah. This course covers the essential manners of a learner alongside a clear breakdown of correct and incorrect beliefs regarding Allah, the Prophets, and the Unseen. Students will also discover the vital roles of the Pious Predecessors (as-Salaf) and the chain of narration (al-Isnad) in understanding and preserving the faith.
